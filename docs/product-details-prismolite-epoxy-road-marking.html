PRISMOLITE EPOXY

Two-component solvent-free epoxy system for heavy-duty pavement marking

PRISMOLITE Epoxy is a two-component solvent-free epoxy road marking system offering exceptional abrasion resistance, chemical resistance, and service life well beyond conventional thermoplastic markings. Engineered for tunnel interiors, bus depots, parking structures, industrial flooring, and high-stress pavement zones, this system cures to a hard, durable film that withstands heavy vehicle loads and harsh chemical exposure. Also used as a high-bond adhesive for road stud installation and pavement marking applications in critical infrastructure projects.
