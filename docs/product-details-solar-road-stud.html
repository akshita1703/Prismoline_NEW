SOLAR ROAD STUD

Self-powered LED delineator visible up to 800 metres in darkness and fog

Our Solar Road Studs are self-powered, LED-active road safety devices that provide high-intensity night-time and low-visibility lane delineation on highways, expressways, and urban roads. Powered by built-in solar panels and lithium batteries, these studs eliminate the need for external power infrastructure, making them ideal for remote road sections, ghat roads, and accident-prone zones. Their active LED output is visible up to 800 metres, substantially improving driver reaction time and reducing accident risk in darkness and fog.
